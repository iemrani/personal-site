title: Global Campaign Localisation Copilot
order: 1
summary: >-
  An LLM application for multi-market marketing operations. It extracts a
  structured campaign spec from a brief and a set of brand guidelines,
  generates channel- and market-specific localised copy, and runs an automated
  brand-compliance check with a human approval queue and a full audit trail.
  Deployed and running on Railway with Postgres. It ships with an interactive
  masterclass, in English and Italian, that explains the architecture and the
  governance decisions to a non-specialist audience.
tech: Python, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, Anthropic SDK, Pydantic, React, Postgres, Railway
